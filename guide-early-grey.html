In the video, I explained that plucking one grey hair doesn't bring ten more, but repeated plucking can damage the follicle.

Genetics is the biggest factor in greying. But if it's happening very early or very fast, some health factors are worth checking.

"Grey hair to a bald spot is not a great upgrade."

WHEN IS IT 'EARLY'?

Greying before about 25 in Indians is usually considered premature. It's worth getting checked if it starts very young, spreads quickly, or comes with other symptoms like tiredness, hair fall or weight changes.

TESTS YOUR DOCTOR MAY ADVISE
Vitamin B12Low B12 is linked with early greying, especially in vegetarians
Thyroid (TSH)Thyroid problems can affect hair colour and texture
CBC and ferritinChecks for anaemia and low iron
Vitamin DLow levels are common and are linked with early greying
WHAT YOU CAN AND CAN'T CHANGE
✔ Can help
✔Correcting a deficiency, if you have one
✔Treating thyroid problems
✔Stopping smoking
✔A balanced diet with enough protein
✕ Can't change
✕Genetics, the biggest factor
✕Hair that has been grey for a long time
✕Age-related greying
MYTHS
✕

"Pluck one, ten will grow"

Each follicle grows one hair. Plucking doesn't affect the follicles around it.

✕

"Plucking is harmless"

Pulling from the same follicle again and again can damage it, and hair may stop growing there.

✕

"An oil will turn grey hair black"

No oil brings back pigment to grey hair.
