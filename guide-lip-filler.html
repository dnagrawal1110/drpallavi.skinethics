THE BASICS
What it isUsually hyaluronic acid, a substance the skin already makes to hold water
Permanent?No. The body breaks it down gradually
Reversible?Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved by a doctor if needed
Why lips look overdoneToo much, too often, or placed in the wrong spot
QUESTIONS TO ASK YOUR DOCTOR
●Are you a qualified doctor, and how often do you do this?
●Which product will you use, and can it be dissolved?
●How much do you suggest, and why?
●What result is realistic for my face?
●What are the risks, and what will you do if something goes wrong?
●What aftercare and follow-up do you offer?
WHO SHOULD NOT HAVE IT
●Pregnant or breastfeeding women
●Anyone with an active cold sore, infection or pimple on the lips
●Anyone with a known allergy to the product
●People on blood thinners or with bleeding problems, unless their doctor clears it
●Anyone who feels pushed into it

Contact your doctor urgently if after filler you notice:

⚠Severe or increasing pain
⚠Skin turning white, grey, blue or blotchy
⚠Swelling that keeps getting worse
⚠Any change in your vision

Mild swelling and bruising for a few days are common. The signs above are rare but need immediate care.
