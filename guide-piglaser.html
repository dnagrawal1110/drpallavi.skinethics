⚡ Smart laser sirf black balloons (melanin) target karta hai — white balloons (normal skin) bilkul safe rehte hain

Pigmentation laser mein ek specific wavelength hoti hai jo sirf melanin (rang) ko identify karti hai. Normal skin tissue ko yeh ignore karta hai — selective destruction.

Selective Photothermolysis — yeh principle hai jis par modern pigmentation lasers kaam karte hain. Heat sirf targeted pigment absorb karta hai — surrounding skin unaffected rehti hai. Burning nahi, precision targeting hai.

Pigmentation Laser Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

1
Specific Wavelength Melanin Ko Target Karta Hai
Laser ki wavelength (e.g., 532nm, 1064nm) specifically melanin pigment absorb karta hai. Yeh wavelength surrounding tissue mein absorb nahi hoti — smart selectivity.
2
Pigment Particles Shatter Hote Hain
Rapid energy pulse melanin particles ko heat karta hai — woh chhote-chhote microscopic fragments mein break ho jaate hain. Yeh process milliseconds mein hota hai.
3
Immune System Clear Karta Hai
Body ke phagocytic cells (macrophages) fragmented melanin particles ko gradually absorb aur remove karte hain. Yeh natural clearing process hai — isliye results gradual aate hain.
4
Session Ke Baad Pigment Fades
Har session ke baad 4-6 weeks mein improvement visible hoti hai jaise immune system clearing karta hai. Multiple sessions ek ek layer se pigmentation address karte hain.

Har Pigmentation Laser Se Theek Nahi Hoti

Sahi diagnosis laser selection se pehle zaroori hai. Alag pigmentation types alag lasers ko respond karti hain:

🟤
Sun Spots / Lentigines
Excellent Response
UV exposure se bane flat brown spots. Q-switched Nd:YAG ya IPL se excellent clearing possible hai.
🔵
Melasma
Needs Care
Hormonal pigmentation — laser helpful hai lekin risk of rebound if done aggressively. Low-fluence laser toning preferred.
⚫
Post-Acne Marks (PIH)
Good Response
Superficial melanin — laser toning + peels combination approach effective hai. Indian skin pe carefully done.
💜
Birthmarks / Nevi
Individual Assessment
Type pe depend karta hai. Kuch respond well, kuch nahi karte. Medical evaluation mandatory before treatment.
Indian skin pe laser — extra care zaroori hai. Higher melanin content means higher risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) if wrong laser or wrong settings used. Expert dermatologist selection non-negotiable hai.

Laser Myths vs Reality

Laser permanently skin ka rang change kar deta hai
Laser existing pigment remove karta hai — new melanocytes normal melanin produce karte rahte hain. Sun protection na ho toh pigment wapas aa sakta hai.
Ek session mein results milenge
Gradual improvement hoti hai. Har session ke baad immune system weeks tak clearing karta hai. Multiple sessions typically needed hain.
Laser ke baad dhoop mein nahi ja sakte hamesha ke liye
Treatment ke baad 4-6 weeks sun protection strict hoti hai. Phir normal SPF routine sufficient hai — permanent restriction nahi.
Sabke liye same laser kaam karta hai
Skin type, pigmentation depth, aur type ke basis par laser selection alag hoti hai. Wrong laser = ineffective ya harmful results.

Before & After Laser — Important Precautions

  • Treatment se 4 weeks pehle strict sun protection shuru karein
  • Active tan pe laser nahi kiya jaata — sun exposure avoid karein
  • Retinol / AHAs 1 week pehle band kar dein
  • Treatment ke baad 7-10 din skin raw/sensitive — gentle products only
  • Post-laser SPF 50+ daily — strict for minimum 4-6 weeks
  • Crust ya scabs ko kabhi pick nahi karein — scarring risk
  • Swimming / steam room / heavy exercise 1 week avoid karein
