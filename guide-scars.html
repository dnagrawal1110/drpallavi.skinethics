Sirf light daalenge toh road theek nahi hogi — lekin agar light construction workers ko signal de ki "yahan repair karna hai"... dheere-dheere naya cement bharna shuru hoga 🏗️

Acne scar laser exactly isi principle par kaam karta hai. Yeh controlled microscopic injury create karta hai — body ise repair signal samajh kar naya collagen banana shuru karti hai. Dheere-dheere scar ki depth kam hone lagti hai.

Patience is the treatment. Ek session mein miracle nahi hota. Collagen ko banne mein weeks aur months lagte hain — isliye acne scar treatment 3-6 mahine ka commitment hai, ek din ka nahi.

Acne Scar Types — Kyunki Sab Ek Jaisi Nahi Hoti

Alag scar types ke liye alag treatment approaches hoti hain. Ek hi treatment sab ke liye nahi chalta:

🗡️
Icepick Scars
Narrow, deep, V-shaped pits — skin mein spike jaisi ghusi hoti hain. Sabse difficult to treat. Typically punched-out extraction ya TCA CROSS technique best respond karti hai.
Best Tx: TCA CROSS + Subcision + RF Microneedling
📦
Boxcar Scars
Wide, flat-bottomed depressions with sharp vertical edges. Chickenpox scars bhi similar look dete hain. Moderate depth.
Best Tx: Fractional Laser + Chemical Peels + Microneedling
🌊
Rolling Scars
Broad, sloping edges — skin undulating ya wavy dikhti hai. Fibrous bands deep tissue se skin ko pull down karte hain.
Best Tx: Subcision (fibrous bands release) + Filler + Microneedling
🏔️
Hypertrophic / Keloid Scars
Raised scars — skin level se upar. Chest aur jawline par zyada common. Completely different treatment approach — laser can worsen keloids.
Best Tx: Intralesional steroids + Silicone sheets + Specific lasers

Acne Scar Treatment Options

Most cases mein combination approach best results deta hai — ek hi treatment se complete clearance rare hai:

⚡
Fractional CO2 Laser
Gold Standard
Deep remodeling. Maximum collagen induction. 5-7 days downtime. Best for moderate-severe atrophic scars.
🪡
RF Microneedling
Minimal Downtime
Radiofrequency + needles = deep collagen stimulation. Safer on Indian skin. 2-3 days mild redness only.
💉
Subcision
For Rolling Scars
Needle fibrous bands ko release karta hai jo skin ko pull down kar rahi hain. Often combined with filler.
🧴
Chemical Peels
Superficial Scars
Glycolic, TCA peels superficial texture aur PIH address karte hain. Good maintenance treatment between laser sessions.

Diet Se Collagen Ko Raw Material Dein

Treatment ke saath-saath diet bhi collagen synthesis support karta hai — yeh often overlooked hai:

🥩
Protein (Eggs, Dal, Chicken, Paneer)
Collagen amino acids — glycine, proline, hydroxyproline — protein se aate hain. Low protein diet = slow healing.
🟢
Vitamin C (Amla, Guava, Bell Peppers)
Collagen synthesis ke liye Vitamin C enzyme co-factor zaroori hai. Deficiency mein collagen production dramatically slow hoti hai.
🥕
Vitamin A (Carrots, Sweet Potato, Leafy Greens)
Skin cell turnover aur fibroblast activity support karta hai — healing mein directly involved.
🌰
Zinc (Pumpkin Seeds, Chickpeas, Nuts)
Wound healing enzyme co-factor. Zinc deficiency post-treatment recovery slow kar sakti hai.
Realistic expectation: Acne scars mein 50-70% improvement excellent result maana jaata hai. 100% clearance possible nahi hota most cases mein. Lekin consistent treatment se visible, significant improvement definitely possible hai.
