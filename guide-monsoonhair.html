Monsoon Mein Kya Ho Sakta Hai

Seborrheic Dermatitis
Malassezia-driven inflammatory scalp condition — thick greasy flakes, intense itching, redness. Chronic condition jo monsoon mein dramatically worsen hoti hai.
Trigger: Malassezia overgrowth + humidity + sebum excess
Folliculitis (Scalp)
Blocked follicles mein bacteria ya Malassezia infection — small painful pustules on scalp. Can cause scarring alopecia if untreated.
Trigger: Sweat + sebum + occlusion + biofilm
Telogen Effluvium (Monsoon Hairfall)
Scalp inflammation → hair follicles prematurely enter resting (telogen) phase → 2-3 months baad heavy shedding. Monsoon mein start, October-November mein peak.
Trigger: Scalp inflammation + nutritional factors + stress

Monsoon Scalp Home Care

  • Shampoo frequency: 2-3x/week max — daily washing sebum strips karta hai
  • Scalp dry rakhein after rain/sweat — towel dry gently, blow dry on cool setting
  • Anti-fungal shampoo (ketoconazole 1%) — prescription grade, 2x/week during monsoon
  • Tea tree oil diluted (5%) — natural anti-fungal, scalp massage 30 min before wash
  • Avoid tight hairstyles in humid weather — scalp breathe karne do
  • Scalp scrub (home version) — sugar + coconut oil + tea tree gentle massage weekly
  • Diet: Zinc + Biotin + Vitamin D — immunity + hair follicle health ke liye

Monsoon Hair Myths

Monsoon mein hairfall normal hai, automatically theek ho jaata hai
Malassezia biofilm-related hairfall self-resolving nahi hota. Untreated scalp inflammation follicle damage kar sakta hai. Early treatment = better outcome.
Zyada shampoo karne se scalp clean hogi, itching jayegi
Over-washing natural oils strip karta hai — sebum rebound → more Malassezia food → worse itching. Less frequent, better quality cleansing needed.
Baalon mein oil lagana monsoon mein achha hai
Heavy oils scalp pe Malassezia ke liye additional sebum/food provide karte hain. Light oils ends pe — scalp pe heavy oiling monsoon mein avoid karein.
