How HIFU Works — The Mechanism

01

Ultrasound Energy Focused at Depth

Like a magnifying glass focuses sunlight to a single point, HIFU converges ultrasound waves to a precise focal point at the target depth — 1.5mm, 3mm, or 4.5mm — without affecting the tissue in between.

02

Thermal Coagulation Points

At the focal point, temperature reaches 60–70°C in a fraction of a second — creating a micro-thermal injury zone (MTZ). The surrounding skin is completely unaffected. Thousands of MTZs are created across the treatment area.

03

Immediate Collagen Contraction

Existing collagen fibres contract immediately in response to the heat — the first phase of tightening that some patients notice within days of treatment.

04

Neocollagenesis Over Months

The thermal injury triggers a wound-healing cascade — fibroblasts produce new collagen to repair the MTZs. This new collagen matures over 3–6 months, progressively lifting and firming the treated area.

4

The Ideal Age Window

Optimal Treatment Range
30 – 50
Early enough that sufficient collagen exists to respond to the stimulus — late enough that laxity is present and meaningful improvement is visible

Before 30, the SMAS is generally tight and collagen is abundant — HIFU produces minimal visible change. After 55–60, severe volume loss and deep structural laxity may make a surgical consultation more appropriate than HIFU alone. Between 30 and 50, HIFU consistently delivers the most satisfying results.

Results — What to Expect and When

Initial Response

Collagen Building
New collagen formation underway. Skin looks slightly fresher. The lifting effect is not yet at peak.
Month 3 – 4
Visible Lifting
The most noticeable improvement phase. Jawline definition, brow lift, and neck tightening become apparent.

Results peak at 6 months. Maintenance sessions every 12–18 months sustain the effect as natural ageing continues.

HIFU is not a replacement for surgery in cases of significant laxity or volume loss. It is an excellent option for those who want real structural improvement without downtime — or who want to delay surgery by maintaining skin quality.
