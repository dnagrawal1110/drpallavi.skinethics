Agar wall par purana kharab paint hai aur uske upar baar-baar naya paint karte rahoge — kabhi smooth nahi dikhegi. Pehle purani damaged layer gently remove karni padti hai. Chemical peel bhi skin ke saath exactly yahi karta hai — controlled way mein upper damaged skin remove karke fresh skin surface pe laata hai.

Controlled, not random. Chemical peel ek medical procedure hai — acid strength, contact time, aur neutralization precisely controlled hoti hai. Professional hands mein yeh safe, predictable, aur effective hai.

Peel Ke Types — Depth Se Decide Hota Hai

Sab peels ek jaisi nahi hoti — depth aur strength skin concern ke hisaab se choose ki jaati hai:

Type
Superficial
Light Peel — Epidermis Only
Mildest peel — sirf top dead cell layer remove karta hai. Zero downtime ya 1-2 days mild flaking. Regular glow maintenance ke liye ideal.
Acids: Glycolic 20-35%, Lactic, Mandelic, Salicylic 20-30%
Type
Medium
Medium Depth — Into Upper Dermis
Pigmentation, moderate acne marks, fine lines address karta hai. 5-7 days peeling + redness. Meaningful results — doctor supervision essential.
Acids: TCA 15-35%, Jessner's + TCA combination
Type
Deep
Deep Peel — Mid Dermis
Severe sun damage, deep wrinkles, significant scarring. Significant downtime 10-14 days. Rarely used on Indian skin (PIH risk). Phenol peels.
Acids: Phenol — used selectively, fair skin only

Chemical Peel Kisko Faida Karta Hai?

🟤
Pigmentation & Dark Spots
Sun damage, melasma, PIH — acid layers pe through keratinocyte shedding pigment uniformly bahar aata hai.
🔴
Acne & Post-Acne Marks
Salicylic peels pores deep clean karte hain. Glycolic/lactic acne marks lighten karte hain. Active + marks dono address.
😶
Dullness & Uneven Texture
Dead cell buildup remove hone se immediate glow. Texture smooth hoti hai. Sebum control improved. Instant visible result.
📏
Fine Lines & Ageing
Medium depth peels collagen stimulate karte hain. Superficial lines soften hoti hain. Part of anti-ageing maintenance protocol.
🌧️
Monsoon Acne (Mandelic)
Mandelic acid peel — humidity-swollen pores ke liye. Antibacterial + exfoliating. Gentle enough for Indian skin in monsoon.
💜
Underarm Pigmentation
Mandelic-arbutin combination peels friction-induced hyperpigmentation address karte hain — sensitive underarm skin ke liye safe.

Recovery — Day by Day

Superficial peel ke baad typical recovery (medium/deep peel mein zyada time):

Day 0
Right After Peel
Mild warmth, redness, tightness — normal. Gentle moisturiser + SPF. No makeup for 24 hours. No touching or washing aggressively.
Days 1–2
Skin Looks Normal or Mildly Dry
Superficial peel: minimal downtime. Medium peel: redness + tight feeling. Moisturiser frequently apply karein. SPF non-negotiable.
Days 3–5
Peeling Begins
Skin shedding start hoti hai — dry flakes ya sheets. Yeh normal aur expected hai — fresh skin aa rahi hai. Peels ko pick nahi karna! Let it shed naturally.
Days 6–7
Fresh Skin Revealed
Peeling complete. Fresh, glowing skin visible. Slight pink tone possible — fades in days. Yeh woh glow hai jiske liye peel ki jaati hai.

Chemical Peel Myths

Chemical peel se skin permanently patli ho jaati hai
Peel damaged dead cells remove karta hai — healthy living cells untouched rehte hain. Long term mein collagen stimulation se skin actually thicker hoti hai.
Darker skin par chemical peel nahi hota
Indian skin pe mandelic, lactic, low-strength glycolic peels safely done jaate hain with proper pre-treatment. Expert selection important hai.
Peel ke baad skin sensitive ho jaati hai hamesha ke liye
Temporary sensitivity hoti hai healing period mein. Once healed — skin actually more resilient hoti hai. Sun protection good habit hai regardless.
Ghar par peel kar sakte hain
OTC very low strength products ≠ professional peel. Clinic-grade peels require proper assessment, neutralization, post-care — unsafe at home.

Apna Pehla Peel — Kaise Prepare Karein

  • Peel se 2 weeks pehle retinol / AHAs band karein
  • Avoid active acne ya open wounds on face
  • No waxing / threading 1 week pehle
  • Sun exposure minimize karein — tan pe peel nahi hota
  • Peel ke din makeup-free aayein, face cleansed
  • Post-peel ke liye gentle cleanser + moisturiser + SPF 50 ready rakhein ghar par
  • Peeling ke dauran gym / swimming / steam avoid karein
Peel ki quality + selection + skin assessment — yeh teeno milke result decide karte hain. Sasta ya parlour peel = unpredictable outcome. Dermatologist-supervised professional peel = safe + effective.
