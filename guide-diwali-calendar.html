YOUR 15-DAY DIWALI SKIN CALENDAR

Festival-Ready Skin,
Planned Day by Day

A simple 15-day plan before Diwali: what to finish early, what to keep steady, and what never to try in the last week.

In the carousel, I explained that the last days before a festival are the wrong time for anything strong or new.

Here is the full calendar. Start wherever you are today. Even a few days of steady, gentle care helps more than one big treatment at the end.

"Before a festival, consistency beats a last-minute fix."
— Dr. Pallavi Ahire-Shelke
THE 15-DAY CALENDAR
Days 15–11

Finish anything strong

If you and your dermatologist have planned a peel or other procedure, this is the latest window, so the skin has time to heal and settle.

Days 10–8

Settle into a simple routine

Gentle cleanser, moisturiser, sunscreen every morning. Keep using what your skin already tolerates.

Days 7–5

No new products

Nothing new on the face from here on, not even a new face pack or serum. A reaction now has no time to calm.

Days 4–3

Protect the barrier

Moisturise well, drink enough water and keep sunscreen on through all the shopping and cleaning.

Days 2–1

Rest and hydrate

No bleach, scrub, wax or facial on the face. Sleep is the best glow treatment you have.

Diwali

Gentle and protected

Cleanse gently at night to remove makeup and smoke. Wear cotton near diyas and fireworks.

NEVER IN THE LAST WEEK
●Bleach or a de-tan pack on the face
●A strong peel or acid you have not used before
●Scrubs, especially on sensitive or acne-prone skin
●A first-time facial or a new parlour treatment
●Waxing or threading the day before an event, if your skin gets red after it
●Any injectable or procedure that was not planned weeks earlier
YOUR DAILY BASICS
MorningGentle cleanse, moisturiser, sunscreen
Through the dayReapply sunscreen if you are outdoors for long
NightCleanse off makeup, sunscreen and smoke, then moisturise
Every daySleep, water and meals at regular times
"The glow you see on Diwali is built in the weeks before it."

Skin that is calm, hydrated and protected looks better than skin recovering from a last-minute treatment.
