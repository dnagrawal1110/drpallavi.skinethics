Harsh Facewash Kyun Kaam Nahi Karta?

1
Skin Barrier Strip Hoti Hai
Harsh cleanser natural lipids remove karta hai — skin ki protective barrier damage hoti hai.
2
Sebum Rebound Effect
Skin "dry" feel hone par sebaceous glands zyada sebum produce karte hain to compensate — result: more oil, more breakouts.
3
pH Imbalance
Healthy skin ka pH 4.5-5.5 hai. Harsh soaps/cleansers yeh balance bigadte hain — acne bacteria zyada thrive karta hai alkaline skin pe.
4
Inflammation Increases
Already inflamed skin pe harsh products — redness aur sensitivity aur badh jaati hai. Breakout heal nahi hote.

Monsoon Skincare Routine

Morning
Gentle foam cleanser (pH 5-5.5) Micellar water ya gel cleanser. Avoid bar soap, sulfate-heavy cleansers.
Toner
Alcohol-free niacinamide toner Pore tightening + sebum control. Avoid alcohol, witch hazel.
Moisturiser
Lightweight gel moisturiser only Water-based, non-comedogenic. Skip heavy creams.
SPF
SPF 30-50 gel sunscreen UV-A penetrates clouds. Sunscreen non-negotiable in monsoon.
Night
Salicylic cleanser + Mandelic/Niacinamide serum 2-3x/week actives. Rest of nights — gentle cleanser + moisturiser only.

Monsoon Acne Myths

Monsoon mein oily skin ko moisturiser nahi chahiye
Har skin type ko hydration chahiye. Dehydrated oily skin more sebum produce karti hai. Lightweight gel moisturiser essential hai.
Baarish ke paani se face wash karna acne theek karta hai
Rainwater air pollutants absorb karta hai. Face wipe with rain water = chemical exposure. Stick to clean water.
Monsoon mein sunscreen ki zaroorat nahi — clouds hain
UV-A (tanning + ageing rays) 80% clouds ke through penetrate karte hain. SPF skipping = sun damage + pigmentation worse hoti hai.
