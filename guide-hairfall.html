Ek Tree Ki Tarah Sochein

Har girta hua baal problem nahi hota. Roz 30 se 100 baal girna normal hair cycle ka hissa ho sakta hai. Isko samajhne ke liye ek tree imagine kijiye:

🌳
Healthy Tree
100 leaves fall
100 new come
Tree hamesha full aur healthy dikhega
🍂
Thinning Tree
100 leaves fall
only 20 return
Dheere-dheere tree patla dikhne lagega

Hair bhi bilkul isi tarah kaam karte hain. Hair fall tab concern banta hai jab naye hairs ugne se zyada hairs girne lagte hain — aur yeh imbalance kisi specific reason se hota hai.

Pillow par 50-80 baal? Panic mat karein — yeh likely normal shedding hai. Concern tab hoga jab density visible reduce ho, hairline recede kare, ya patches form hon.

Normal Hair Fall — Kitna Hona Chahiye?

Medically Accepted Normal Range
50 – 100
baal per din
Wash days par 150-200 bhi normal ho sakta hai — kyunki kuch shed hairs washing tak rukke hote hain.

Lekin sirf count karna accurate nahi hai. Zyada important yeh hai: kya aapki overall density kam ho rahi hai? Kya ponytail patli ho rahi hai? Kya scalp parts mein zyada dikhne laga hai? Yeh visual cues zyada reliable hain.

Hair Growth Cycle — Teen Phases

Har single hair apne life mein teen phases se guzarta hai — simultaneously, alag alag hairs alag phases mein hote hain:

Phase 1
🌱
85%
Anagen — Growth Phase
Hair actively grow kar raha hai. Follicle fully alive aur nourished hai. Ek mahine mein approximately 1 cm growth hoti hai. Yeh phase jitna lamba hoga, hair utna lamba grow kar sakta hai.
Duration: 2 – 7 years (genetics pe depend)
Phase 2
⏸️
1–2%
Catagen — Transition Phase
Hair growth ruk jaati hai. Follicle shrink hoti hai aur hair shaft se disconnect hone lagti hai. Yeh ek short transitional period hai.
Duration: 2 – 3 weeks
Phase 3
😴
10–15%
Telogen — Rest Phase
Hair resting state mein hai — follicle dormant. Naya hair neeche se grow hone lagta hai. Yeh phase ke end mein purana hair naturally shed hota hai.
Duration: 3 – 4 months
Phase 4
🍂
Shedding
Exogen — Shedding Phase
Telogen hair actively scalp se detach hota hai — yahi woh baal hain jo comb mein dikhte hain. Naya anagen hair isi follicle se ugna shuru karta hai.
Normal: 50–100 baal per day shed hote hain is phase mein

Hair Fall Ke Common Causes

Har patient mein cause alag hota hai — isliye treatment bhi alag hogi. Yeh sabse common reasons hain:

🧬
Genetic / Androgenetic
Male-pattern ya female-pattern hair loss. DHT hormone follicles ko miniaturize karta hai. Alag treatment approach chahiye.
😰
Physical / Mental Stress
Telogen effluvium — stress ke 2-3 mahine baad sudden shedding. Illness, surgery, ya major life events ke baad common.
🩸
Nutritional Deficiency
Iron (ferritin), Vitamin D, B12, Zinc, Protein — inki deficiency directly hair growth cycle disrupt karti hai.
⚖️
Hormonal Changes
PCOS, thyroid (hypo/hyper), post-pregnancy, menopause — yeh sab hair cycle ko significantly affect karte hain.
🤒
Post-Illness Shedding
COVID-19, dengue, typhoid ke baad 2-3 mahine mein sudden hair fall — yeh temporary telogen effluvium hota hai, regrows naturally.
🔬
Scalp Conditions
Dandruff (seborrheic dermatitis), psoriasis, alopecia areata — scalp inflammation follicles ko damage karti hai.

Lifestyle Factors Jo Matter Karte Hain

😴
Sleep Quality
Growth hormone sleep mein release hota hai — follicle repair raat ko hoti hai. Chronic sleep deprivation hair quality aur growth rate dono ko impact karta hai.
🥗
Protein Intake
Hair keratin — protein se bana hota hai. Low-protein diet (especially crash diets) mein 2-3 mahine baad significant shedding hoti hai.
🔥
Heat Styling
Daily blow-dry, straightening, curling — heat hair shaft weaken karta hai aur breakage badhti hai. Yeh hair fall nahi hai — breakage hai. Lekin result wahi dikhta hai.
💊
Medications
Blood thinners, antidepressants, certain BP medications, high-dose Vitamin A — yeh sab hair shedding ka side effect de sakte hain.
🧴
Tight Hairstyles
Tight ponytails, braids, buns — traction alopecia create karte hain jahan hairline aur temples se permanent follicle damage ho sakti hai.

Hair Fall Myths — Jo Avoid Karein

Zyada champi (oiling) se hair fall rukta hai
Oil scalp hair fall cause nahi karta, na rokta hai. Follicle issues andar se hote hain — oil sirf surface pe hota hai.
Baal zyada wash karne se fall hote hain
Washing sirf shed hone wale baalo ko nikaalta hai jo vaise bhi girne the. Daily washing hair fall ka cause nahi hai.
Biotin supplement se hair fall rukta hai
Sirf agar genuine biotin deficiency ho (rare hai) tabhi kaam karta hai. Most hair fall ka biotin se koi connection nahi.
Yeh temporary hai, khud theek ho jaayega
Kuch types (telogen effluvium) temporary hote hain, kuch (androgenetic) progressive hote hain. Without diagnosis — aap guess kar rahe hain.

Kab Dermatologist Ke Paas Jaayein?

  • 3 mahine se zyada heavy shedding chal rahi hai
  • Scalp par patches — circular bald spots (alopecia areata possible)
  • Hairline recede ho rahi hai ya temples patla ho rahe hain
  • Ponytail diameter noticeably reduce ho gayi hai
  • Scalp par itching, scaling, ya redness ke saath hair fall
  • Post-pregnancy 6 mahine ke baad bhi fall nahi ruka
  • Family history hai — early treatment better outcomes deta hai
Sabse important: Hair fall ka cause identify kiye bina treatment start karna — guessing hai. Blood tests (ferritin, thyroid, Vitamin D, CBC) + scalp examination = accurate diagnosis → sahi treatment.
