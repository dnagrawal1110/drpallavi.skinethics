The Garden Transplant Analogy

Yeh sabse common misconception hai: "Hair transplant mein naye baal banaye jaate hain." Bilkul nahi.

🌿🌿🌿
Donor Area
Permanent, DHT-resistant follicles — occipital scalp
➡️
🕳️🕳️🕳️
Recipient Area
Thinning / bald zones — frontal scalp

Bilkul waisi hi jaise garden ke peeche se healthy plants nikalkar saamne ki khaali jagah mein lagaate hain — hum existing permanent hair roots ko relocate karte hain, naye create nahi karte.

Isliye donor area quality sabse important hai. Agar donor area mein kaafi healthy, DHT-resistant follicles nahi hain — transplant ka scope limited hota hai. Yeh sabse pehle assess kiya jaata hai consultation mein.

Donor Baal Permanent Kyun Hote Hain?

Scalp ke peeche aur sides ke baal — occipital aur temporal zone — genetically DHT (dihydrotestosterone) ke against resistant hote hain. DHT woh hormone hai jo androgenetic alopecia mein follicles ko miniaturize karta hai.

Jab in resistant follicles ko thinning area mein transplant kiya jaata hai — woh apni genetic memory saath le jaate hain. Naye location mein bhi yeh DHT ke against resistant rehte hain. Isliye transplanted baal permanent hote hain.

Lekin — non-transplanted native baal thinning continue kar sakte hain. Isliye hair loss stabilize karna (medication se) transplant ke saath-saath zaroori hota hai.

FUE vs FUT — Kya Fark Hai?

FUE
Follicular Unit Extraction
• Individual follicles extract kiye jaate hain
• No linear scar — punktate micro-scars only
• Short hair mein bhi donor area natural dikhta hai
• Recovery faster — 7-10 days
• Preferred technique today
FUT
Follicular Unit Transplant
• Strip of scalp removed from donor
• Linear scar at donor site
• More grafts in one session
• Recovery 10-14 days
• Used when large graft count needed

Kaun Achha Candidate Hai?

  • Stable hair loss (2+ years se zyada nahi badha) — Norwood Grade 3–6
  • Sufficient donor area density — occipital zone mein thick baal
  • Realistic expectations — density improvement, complete restoration nahi
  • Age 25+ — younger patients mein loss pattern still evolving hoti hai
  • Good overall health — no active autoimmune conditions
  • Active alopecia areata — transplant iss condition mein recommended nahi
  • Diffuse unpatterned alopecia — donor area bhi thin hai
  • Unrealistic expectation of "original density" — ethical doctor refuse karega

Month-by-Month Recovery Timeline

Transplant ke baad patience most important hai — results instant nahi hote. Yeh expected progression hai:

Days
1–10
Post-Op Recovery
Scabs form hoti hain transplanted area mein. Mild swelling aur redness normal. Gentle washing day 3 se shuru. Rest important — no strenuous activity.
Weeks
2–6
Shock Loss — Don't Panic
Transplanted baal shed ho jaate hain — yeh bilkul normal hai. "Shock loss" kehte hain ise. Follicle alive hai, sirf hair shaft gg gaya — naya aayega.
Month
3–4
New Growth Begins
Thin, fine baal dikhne lagte hain. Texture initially different lag sakta hai — yeh gradually improves. Itching is common — sign of growth.
Month
6–8
Significant Density Visible
50-60% of final result visible by now. Baal thicker aur darker ho rahe hain. Most patients is stage par result se excited hote hain.
Month
12–18
Final Result
Full maturation — baal naturally thick aur textured. Hairline fully settled. Ab styling possible. This is the result you compare against pre-op photos.

Hairline Design — Utna Hi Important Hai

Ek technically perfect transplant bhi galat hairline design se unnatural dikh sakta hai. Achhi hairline design:

Natural hairline kabhi perfectly straight nahi hoti. Subtle irregularities, micro-irregularities, aur temple points — yeh sab natural appearance create karte hain. Experienced surgeon ka artistic eye technically skilled hands se kam important nahi hota.

Age-appropriate design bhi zaroori hai — 25 saal ke patient aur 45 saal ke patient ki hairline same nahi honi chahiye. Future hair loss progression ko bhi hairline planning mein factor karna padta hai.

Hair Transplant Myths

Transplant ke baad baal permanently dense rahenge
Transplanted baal permanent hain, lekin native non-transplanted baal continue thinning kar sakte hain. Medication (minoxidil/finasteride) transplant ke saath zaroori hoti hai.
Transplant mein bahut dard hota hai
Procedure local anaesthesia mein hota hai — patient awake hote hain lekin pain nahi hoti. Post-op mild discomfort 2-3 days medication se manage hota hai.
Ek session mein poora result milega
Advanced baldness mein 2 sessions ki zaroorat ho sakti hai. Grafts ki limit per session hoti hai — overharvesting donor area damage karta hai.
